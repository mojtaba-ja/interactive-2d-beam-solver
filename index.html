Values at a section

Sign convention — downward forces and clockwise moments are positive on input; shear is the resultant of the upward forces to the left of the cut; bending moment is positive when it sags (tension on the bottom fibre); deflection is positive upward. Analysis is Euler–Bernoulli (bending only, shear deformation neglected), small displacements, linear elastic.
